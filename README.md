# Ore Cutter

A Fabric and NeoForge mod (and a plain data pack) for Minecraft 26.3. **Craft deepslate ores, and swap any ore with its deepslate twin in the stonecutter.**

Every ore you own, in stone or deepslate, whenever you like. Craft a deepslate ore from a block of
its material and a piece of deepslate, or put an ore block in the stonecutter and take out its twin.

## What it does

- **Craft deepslate ores.** A block of the material plus one deepslate, anywhere in the crafting
  grid, makes the deepslate ore: block of coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, emerald, lapis or
  diamond. For copper, iron and gold, the smelted block and the raw block both work.
- **Swap in the stonecutter.** Coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, emerald, lapis and diamond ore
  each cut to their deepslate twin, and each deepslate ore cuts back. One block in, one block out.
- **Fair by design.** A crafted ore costs a whole block, so diamonds, emeralds, iron and gold stay
  as precious as you found them.
- **Pure vanilla feel.** It adds recipes to the crafting table and stonecutter you already own,
  and that's all.
- **Server friendly.** Install it on the server and everyone can use the recipes.

## Which file?

- **Fabric:** `ore-cutter-fabric-<version>.jar`, with [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
- **NeoForge:** `ore-cutter-neoforge-<version>.jar`.
- **Playing without a mod loader:** `ore-cutter-<version>-datapack.zip` goes in your world's
  `datapacks` folder. It holds the same recipes.
